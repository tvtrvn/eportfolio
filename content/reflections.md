# Reflections

<p class="lede">A reflection is more than a summary of what happened. Each one here is attached to an artifact in this portfolio and works through three steps.</p>

<div class="table-scroll" markdown="1">

| Step | The question it answers |
|---|---|
| **Retell** | What happened? A short account of the experience, project or task. |
| **Relate** | How does it connect to what I'm learning in LE/COOP 2100, and which ideas or frameworks apply? |
| **Reflect** | What did I learn about myself, what would I do differently, and how has my thinking changed? |

</div>

Every reflection also names the Lassonde undergraduate competency it demonstrates, and explains why.
