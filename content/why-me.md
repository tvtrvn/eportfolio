## In the video

- **Who I am:** a Computer Science co-op student at York University's Lassonde School of Engineering, graduating in 2028, looking for a Summer 2027 co-op in software engineering.
- **Why I do what I do:** a friend since elementary school whose perseverance inspired me to switch from nursing to computer science.
- **What I build:** AI training for teams at Groupe Bel Canada, the [online ordering site](https://gingercuisine.ca/) for my family's restaurant, and a [truth-first resume pipeline](https://github.com/tvtrvn/truth-first-resume-pipeline) on GitHub.
